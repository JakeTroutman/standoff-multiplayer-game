How to play

Both players choose secretly. Actions reveal together. Start with 3 health and 1 bullet; caps are 3 health and 2 bullets. First to 2 duel wins takes the match.

Shoot: spend a bullet to deal 1 damage. Reload: gain a bullet, even if shot. Dodge: avoid all damage; the shooter still spends their bullet.

Use an action at most twice in a row. Both shooting deals damage to both. Both falling together is a draw with no win awarded. There’s no timer. Each new duel resets health, ammo, and action streaks.
